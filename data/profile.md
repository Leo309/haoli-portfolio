## What Hao does

Hao's work falls into three kinds:

1. **He builds AI tools and products.** GeoGrade, for example, is an AI search-visibility audit for local businesses that he built on Claude Managed Agents and an MCP server he wrote.
2. **He automates recurring work.** Examples include cold-email follow-up workflows for DTC brands, n8n pipelines that turn TikTok trends into ad storyboards, and Power Automate flows that refresh reporting without anyone clicking a button.
3. **He delivers for clients.** Two examples:
   - A data platform strategy and roadmap for a knitwear manufacturer with over $100M in revenue, adopted by leadership.
   - A custom CRM that replaced spreadsheets and saved each client more than 10 hours a month.

His foundation is analytics engineering: SQL, data modeling, pipelines and BI. That background is what makes the AI work practical, because an agent is only as useful as the data it can reach.

## Why Hao builds

A team down the hall from Hao's once mentioned it was time to renew their software subscription again. It cost several thousand dollars a year, for a tool that did a simple data transformation, and no one on the team had the skills to replace it. Hao spent a few hours writing a small Python tool to replace it, packaged so the team could run it themselves, and the team cancelled the subscription.

That's when it clicked for him. Most businesses don't need more software. They need someone who can build the small thing that fits, and AI makes that faster and cheaper than ever.

## How Hao thinks about AI

Every technology investment comes down to two outcomes: more revenue or lower costs. Hao's approach has three steps:

1. He starts from the business problem, not the technology.
2. He picks the simplest thing that works. Sometimes that's an agent and sometimes it's a spreadsheet formula.
3. He measures the result.

His own site's agent, AskHao, is an example. It started as a RAG system. When the knowledge base turned out small enough to give the model in full, he removed the retrieval layer and used long context with prompt caching and citations instead.
