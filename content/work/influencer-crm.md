## The problem

DTC brand owners were tracking their influencer partnerships in Excel. Statuses, contacts and deliverables lived in spreadsheets that took hours to keep up to date.

## What I did

I built a CRM made for that job, from 0 to 1:

- Excel import and export, plus bulk operations, so onboarding was fast
- Status updates, grouping and structured records instead of free-form cells

It later became CollabZen, a partner management product, which is now paused.

## The result

- **10+ hours** of manual work saved per client, every month
- Metadata for influencers with **10M+** total followers, in one place
